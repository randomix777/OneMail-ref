<Note>
The iOS and Android apps are **coming soon**. They are not in the app stores yet, and this page is here so you can see what is planned instead of finding an empty download.
</Note>

OneMail is a mail client first. The web app and the desktop app (macOS and Windows) are that client today; the mobile apps will be the same client in your pocket, on the same account, with the same mailboxes and the same assistant.

## What is planned

- **The same inbox, on your phone.** Every connected mailbox in one view, with the categories and the "To reply" list the assistant has already applied.
- **The assistant with you.** Ask what needs an answer, get a summary, and send a reply without waiting to get back to a laptop.
- **Rules you can change on the go.** Write a rule in plain language, or edit, pause and delete the ones you have.
- **Verification codes pushed to you.** When a code lands in your inbox, it can be pushed straight to your phone.

## What to use meanwhile

- The web app at [jyisolutions.com](https://www.jyisolutions.com): the full client and the assistant.
- The desktop app from the [download page](https://www.jyisolutions.com/app) for macOS and Windows.
- Slack, Telegram and Microsoft Teams, so the assistant can reach you while you are away. See [Channels](/essentials/channels).

## Signing in

The mobile apps will sign in with your OneMail account, the same one you use on the web. There is nothing to set up in advance.
